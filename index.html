Workshop on Uncertain Dynamical Systems (WUDS2026)

Date: August 18 (Tue) - 20 (Thu), 2026

Venue: Umeda Campus, Osaka Institute of Technology, Osaka, Japan

About the Workshop

The workshop on Uncertain Dynamical Systems (WUDS) is an international event organized by the Technical Committee on Robust Control of the International Federation of Automatic Control (IFAC). The workshop traditionally takes place the week before the IFAC World Congress, a flagship event of the IFAC. The 2026 IFAC World Congress will take place in Busan, Republic of Korea, from August 23 to 28, 2026. The 2026 WUDS will be organized in the preceding week, from August 18 to 20, 2026, in Osaka, Japan.

The goal of the WUDS is to gather a selected number of internationally renowned researchers with a robust control background in an event characterized by a friendly atmosphere, where they can share their most recent cutting-edge research results on advanced and applied control topics. The 2026 WUDS will be held in close collaboration with the project JST ASPIRE “Building Mathematical Foundation for Cyber Physical Dynamical Systems: Interdisciplinary Research and Human Resource Development on Control with Prediction and Learning,” whose abbreviation is “ASPIRE-CPDS.”

Information for Poster Presenters

Each poster presenter will be provided with one poster board. The available display area on each board is approximately 115 cm wide and 169 cm high. A portrait-oriented A0 poster can be displayed on the board.

Please note that the organizers will not print posters for presenters. Poster presenters are requested to either bring their printed posters with them or arrange poster printing near the venue.

For example, poster printing services are available at Kinko's Umeda Store near the venue. Please note that the linked page is in Japanese. You may also search for “Kinko's Umeda Store” on Google Maps.
